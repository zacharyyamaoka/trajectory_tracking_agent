What the tests exercise

A read-only NumPy array can be made writable by its owner. The controller previously received the same message used by scoring. The evaluator now copies the sample data into immutable buffers for the controller.

  • Controller input. The clock, joint samples, and sample channels are read-only. Direct writes and attempts to enable writes fail the completion gate.
  • Scoring. The simulator and scorer keep the original tape. A half-speed controller is scored on its original timestamps and positions.
  • Host loop. A valid controller receives joint states at 50 Hz. A wrong return type or missing joint fails the completion gate.
