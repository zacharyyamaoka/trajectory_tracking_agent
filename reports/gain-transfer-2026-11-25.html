What changed in the fit

The candidate used the same parent model, dev records, gravity and friction bases, and ridge penalty as the PD-only fit. It added the given model’s mass matrix times acceleration error to the fit target. The resulting weights were identical in the two candidates.

The committed prediction was at least 10% less error at configured gains and a stiff-gain score within 5% of its reference. Only the stiff-gain prediction held. The existing model fitted at configured gains is still better at 0.06991 mm.
