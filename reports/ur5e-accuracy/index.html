What ran

The stock arm_motion_bench PEP 0032 portal fixture has a static belt, four bins and a capsule that stands in for the claw. The arm carried no payload. The controller used the mount orientation to correct the sign of the gravity term. These are simulation results. Physical part pickup and the dynamic Robotiq gripper remain unmeasured.
