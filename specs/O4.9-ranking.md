# O4.9 Ranking

## Requirement

> **Ranking:** admissible solutions by `tool_track_rms_mm` (lower first); then the rest by gate pass rate, then
> objective. The *best* solution is the lowest-objective admissible one.

From [OBJECTIVES.md §4 The score](../OBJECTIVES.md#4-the-score), ruler v1. OBJECTIVES.md is the frozen ruler and the one source of every number; this note explains it and never overrides it.

## What it means

Admissible solutions come first, best objective first. The rest follow by how many of their task gates pass, then by
objective. The best solution is the admissible one with the lowest objective; when no solution is admissible, there is
no best, and the progress log says so.

Solutions an ablation study made (tagged `ablation`) are measurements of a finished line's parts, not search steps:
the best, PROGRESS.md and status.json leave them out (see the feedback below).

## Why

Gates first, objective second: a fast controller that breaks a gate is not better than a slower one that passes them
all. Ablation variants stay out of the best because "ablation variants must not read as search progress"
(Zach, 2026-11-24, as `tools/progress_chart.py` quotes the rule). Filed as feedback:
[ablations are not progress](../feedback/2026-11-24-ablations-are-not-progress.md).

## How it is checked

- `test_ranking_is_objectives_section_4`: a ledger of admissible and failing solutions ranks in this order, and the
  best is the admissible one with the lowest objective.
- `test_no_admissible_solution_is_said_plainly`: no admissible solution means no best, said in words.
