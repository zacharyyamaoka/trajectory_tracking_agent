# O2.1 Deployment ur5e_alone

## Requirement

> | Part | Value | Source |
> |---|---|---|
> | Robot + plant | MuJoCo Menagerie UR5e, the "truth" plant (armature, dry friction, damping), floor-mounted | `robot_controllers.bench.ur5e` (`Ur5ePlant`, `UR5E_TRUTH`) |
> | Servo boards | the moteus board model with the UR5e datasheet caps (150 ×3, 28 ×3 N·m) | `robot_controllers.bench.ur5e.ur5e_board_config()` |

From [OBJECTIVES.md §2 Deployment v1: `ur5e_alone`](../OBJECTIVES.md#2-deployment-v1-ur5e_alone), ruler v1. OBJECTIVES.md is the frozen ruler and the one source of every number; this note explains it and never overrides it.

## What it means

One fixed simulated world. The arm is MuJoCo Menagerie's UR5e with a "truth" plant (armature, dry friction, damping)
that no solution may read ([O1.2](O1.2-what-a-solution-may-know.md)). Each joint sits behind a moteus board model
carrying the UR5e's datasheet torque caps. The host ticks at 50 Hz and its commands travel a bus timed from the
datasheet, so a command lands late the way it would on the rig. The arm stands alone on a floor, and the tool point is
the flange site.

All of it is imported from robot_controllers at a pinned version, so the ruler cannot drift through a local edit.

## Why

Two benches once scored the same UR5e about 1000× apart because they drew the boundary in different places
(OBJECTIVES.md's WHY, quoted in [O1.1](O1.1-the-boundary.md)); owning the rate and the bus in the deployment, never in
the solution, is half of drawing it once.

The scene is Zach's: "just the UR", "UR with nothing around it" (2026-11-23, quoted in
`datasets/ur5e_alone/workspace/v2/station.json`). Filed as feedback:
[just the UR](../feedback/2026-11-23-just-the-ur.md).

## How it is checked

- `test_deployment_is_objectives_section_2`: the deployment's host rate, bus, board model, caps, floor and limits are
  this table's.
- `test_the_mount_matches_the_datasets_floor_mount_up_to_a_yaw`: the plant stands where the dataset's stored tool
  positions say the arm stood (the same base on the same floor, turned about the vertical axis).
- `test_link_points_put_the_base_on_the_floor_and_the_tool_last`: the points the floor gate reads start at the base on
  the floor and end at the tool point.
- `tests/test_ruler_determinism.py::test_the_deployment_calibration_is_the_pinned_one`: the deployment's calibrated
  lookahead is the pinned robot_controllers value, so a dependency bump that moves it is caught.
