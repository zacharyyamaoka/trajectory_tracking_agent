# O5.7 Camera-measured tool point

## Requirement

> Deferred to ruler v2 (named so nobody thinks they were forgotten)
>
> a camera-measured tool point

From [OBJECTIVES.md §5 Deferred to ruler v2](../OBJECTIVES.md#5-deferred-to-ruler-v2-named-so-nobody-thinks-they-were-forgotten), ruler v1. OBJECTIVES.md is the frozen ruler and the one source of every number; this note explains it and never overrides it.

## What it means

Today the measured tool point is the FK of the joint encoders ([O4.1](O4.1-objective-tool-track-rms.md)). A camera
would see where the tool really is, including what bends between the joints and the tool, which the FK cannot.

## Why

Named in §5 "so nobody thinks they were forgotten". [ideas.md](../ideas.md)'s *Suggestions for Zach* asks for link
and gearbox compliance in the twin for ruler v2; until the twin bends, encoder FK and a camera would agree.

## How it is checked

Deferred: not part of ruler v1, so nothing tests it and its status is *deferred*. It becomes a requirement only in ruler v2, which OBJECTIVES.md says is a new section and a re-score of every solution in [`solutions/`](../solutions/).
