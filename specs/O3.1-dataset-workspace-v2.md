# O3.1 Dataset workspace/v2

## Requirement

> - The UR5e's whole workspace, arm alone on the floor: joint A→B moves, straight tool lines, via points and the two
>   ISO 9283 tasks. **dev** = seed 1001 (42 tasks), **holdout** = seed 2001 (12 tasks).
> - Timed by bam_ruckig (jerk-limited, time-optimal) at the UR5e's limits. **Speed 1.0 only** in ruler v1.
> - Frozen copy of arm_motion_bench [`datasets/ur5e_alone/workspace/v2`](../datasets/ur5e_alone/workspace/v2/) (its `manifest.json` sha256s), with the timed
>   tapes at speed 1.0 committed (bam_ruckig is private, so the public repo ships tapes, not the generator).
>   `tta verify` re-checks every sha256.

From [OBJECTIVES.md §3 The dataset v1: `ur5e_alone/workspace/v2`](../OBJECTIVES.md#3-the-dataset-v1-ur5e_aloneworkspacev2), ruler v1. OBJECTIVES.md is the frozen ruler and the one source of every number; this note explains it and never overrides it.

## What it means

The targets are fixed files. The dev split (42 tasks) is what a solution runs and is scored on; the holdout split
(12 tasks) is scored only to report a finished solution ([O4.8](O4.8-holdout-is-reporting-only.md)). Every tape was
timed once, jerk-limited and time-optimal at the UR5e's limits, at speed 1.0, and committed; nothing here re-times them.

## Why

A frozen copy of the tapes arm_motion_bench scores means a number here and a number there measure the same motions.
The tapes are committed rather than regenerated because the generator, bam_ruckig, is private and this repo is
public. A tape that changed by one byte would silently change every score, so every sha256 is re-checked.

## How it is checked

- `test_verify_*`: `tta verify`'s dataset check is clean, and it catches a changed tape, a new or a missing file, and a
  manifest rewritten to match a changed tape (each tape's sha256 must also be the one arm_motion_bench's manifest lists).
- `test_split_counts_and_order`: dev and holdout hold their task counts, in order.
- `test_every_tape_is_in_ur5e_joint_order_from_rest`: every tape is in the UR5e's joint order, starts and ends at rest,
  and carries no feed-forward (a solution brings its own).
