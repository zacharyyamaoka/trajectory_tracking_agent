# O4.1 Objective: tool_track_rms_mm

## Requirement

> **Objective (one number, lower is better): `tool_track_rms_mm`** = the median over dev tasks of each task's RMS
> distance between the measured tool point and the target tool point *at the same instant*, over the task's moving
> samples. Both tool points come from the joint encoders through the same FK (§2's tool point), never from simulator
> body poses. *Moving* = target tool speed > 1 mm/s or any target joint speed > 0.06 °/s (arm_motion_bench `kpi_names`).

From [OBJECTIVES.md §4 The score](../OBJECTIVES.md#4-the-score), ruler v1. OBJECTIVES.md is the frozen ruler and the one source of every number; this note explains it and never overrides it.

## What it means

For each dev task: at every target sample where the target is moving, take the distance between where the tool is (the
FK of the measured joints) and where it should be at that same instant (the FK of the target joints); the task's
number is the RMS of those distances, in millimetres. The objective is the median of the task numbers.

- *At the same instant*: lagging behind the target is error, even on the right path.
- *Moving samples only*: a long rest before or after a move does not dilute the number.
- *Median*: one odd task does not decide the score; every task still has to pass every gate
  ([O4.7](O4.7-fail-closed-admissible.md)).
- *Through the FK of the joints*: the measurement a real arm's encoders give, never a simulator body pose a real arm
  doesn't have.

## Why

One number makes the search a search: lower is better, and nothing else ranks a solution
([O4.9](O4.9-ranking.md), [O4.10](O4.10-diagnostics-never-optimized.md)). How it is calculated is fixed for the same
reason the boundary is:

> WHY frozen (Zach, 2026-11-24): *"BEFORE I even take a look at kind what is going on inside … finalize the API, what is
> going in and out, what are the evaluation metrics, and how are they calculated."* The arm_motion_bench contract was
> amended 16 times in four days (v3.1 → v3.16), and two benches scored the same UR5e about 1000× apart
> (< 0.05 mm vs 2.0 mm), because they drew the boundary in different places. One frozen ruler fixes both.

Filed as feedback: [freeze the ruler first](../feedback/2026-11-24-freeze-the-ruler-first.md).

## How it is checked

- `test_moving_mask_*`: what counts as moving, just above and just below each threshold, by tool speed alone and by
  joint speed alone.
- `test_tool_track_*`: millimetres out of metres in; only moving samples count; resting samples do not dilute; a task
  with no moving samples is NaN.
- `test_objective_*`: the median of odd and even counts; any NaN makes it NaN; no tasks is NaN.
- `test_metrics_compose_on_rest_move_rest`: the metrics agree on one rest, move, rest motion.
- [`tests/test_ruler_oracle.py`](../tests/test_ruler_oracle.py): an independent oracle. The episode is re-run straight through robot_controllers, the
  tool pose comes from MuJoCo's own site rather than the ruler's FK, the error from robot_msgs, and the result must
  agree with the ruler's objective.
- `test_measured_tool_is_fk_of_the_plants_joints_on_the_targets_grid`: both tool points are the deployment's FK of
  joint angles, on the target's own instants.
