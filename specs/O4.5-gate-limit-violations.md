# O4.5 Gate: limit violations = 0

## Requirement

> | `limit_violations` | 0 samples outside a position limit or above 1.05 × a velocity limit | — |

From [OBJECTIVES.md §4 The score](../OBJECTIVES.md#4-the-score), ruler v1. OBJECTIVES.md is the frozen ruler and the one source of every number; this note explains it and never overrides it.

## What it means

No sample may put a joint outside its position limits or move it faster than 1.05 × its velocity limit. A joint
exactly on a position limit, or exactly at 1.05 × its velocity limit, does not count.

## Why

OBJECTIVES.md gives no reason (its Why cell is "—"). A reading, not the ruler's: a real arm's own controller faults on
these, so a solution that crosses them would not run there; the headroom on velocity leaves room for a tracking
overshoot on tapes timed right at the UR5e's limits ([O3.1](O3.1-dataset-workspace-v2.md)).

## How it is checked

- `test_limit_*`: on the limit does not count and outside does; above 1.05 × counts, between the limit and 1.05 × does
  not; each joint against its own limits; samples are counted, not violations.
- `test_gate_limit_violations*`: the gate passes only at zero.
