# O1.1 The boundary

## Requirement

> **The boundary is robot_msgs `JointTrajectory` + `JointState` in, `ServoCommand` out** (robot_controllers' contract
> §2), at the deployment's host rate over its bus. A moteus board takes a `ServoCommand` as is; a UR servoJ stream is
> one with position only; UR's direct torque is one with feed-forward only. The solution may use any of those fields.

From [OBJECTIVES.md §1 The black box](../OBJECTIVES.md#1-the-black-box), ruler v1. OBJECTIVES.md is the frozen ruler and the one source of every number; this note explains it and never overrides it.

## What it means

A solution is one function, `build(trajectory, deployment)`, that returns a controller. Every host tick the controller
is handed the arm's `JointState` and returns a `ServoCommand` (and a dict of its own diagnostics). Those are the only
two doors: everything the solution learns about the arm comes in as joint states, and everything it does goes out as a
servo command, in the message types a moteus board or a UR arm already takes. A controller that wins here is wired the
way a real arm is, so moving it to one changes no interface.

The rate and the bus belong to the deployment ([O2.1](O2.1-deployment-ur5e-alone.md)): a solution decides *what* to
send each tick, never *when* the tick runs or how long its command takes to arrive.

## Why

> WHY frozen (Zach, 2026-11-24): *"BEFORE I even take a look at kind what is going on inside … finalize the API, what is
> going in and out, what are the evaluation metrics, and how are they calculated."* The arm_motion_bench contract was
> amended 16 times in four days (v3.1 → v3.16), and two benches scored the same UR5e about 1000× apart
> (< 0.05 mm vs 2.0 mm), because they drew the boundary in different places. One frozen ruler fixes both.

That is OBJECTIVES.md's own WHY for the frozen ruler. Drawing the boundary once, at the messages a real arm takes, is
what makes two scores comparable. Zach's words are filed as feedback:
[freeze the ruler first](../feedback/2026-11-24-freeze-the-ruler-first.md).

## How it is checked

**No test proves it yet** (`tests: []`, so its status is *unverified*).

The evaluator enforces it in code: `evaluate.py` wraps the controller, and a `tick` that returns anything but a
`(ServoCommand, diagnostics)` pair, or a command that leaves out one of the deployment's joints, raises, which fails
`completed` ([O4.2](O4.2-gate-completed.md)). The loop that calls `tick` is robot_controllers' `run_episode`, at the
deployment's host rate over its bus. No test hands the evaluator a controller that breaks the boundary, and none checks
the rate a solution is ticked at. Writing that test is in [ideas.md](../ideas.md) under *I'll try*.
