# O4.4 Gate: saturation ≤ 2 %

## Requirement

> | `saturation_share_pct` | ≤ 2 %: share of samples where any joint torque is ≥ 98 % of its cap | speed bought with saturation doesn't transfer |

From [OBJECTIVES.md §4 The score](../OBJECTIVES.md#4-the-score), ruler v1. OBJECTIVES.md is the frozen ruler and the one source of every number; this note explains it and never overrides it.

## What it means

Count the samples in which any joint's torque, by magnitude, is at or above 98 % of that joint's cap (the datasheet
caps of [O2.1](O2.1-deployment-ur5e-alone.md)); that share of the run must be at most 2 %. The evaluator counts it on
every physics sample, not only the target's, so a spike between two target samples still counts.

## Why

"Speed bought with saturation doesn't transfer" (OBJECTIVES.md §4). A controller that rides the torque cap can track
well in a twin that knows the cap exactly; on a real arm it has no margin left for whatever the model got wrong.

## How it is checked

- `test_saturation_*`: exactly 0.98 of the cap counts and just under does not; a negative torque counts by magnitude;
  each joint against its own cap; a sample counts once whichever joint saturates.
- `test_gate_saturation*`: the gate's threshold.
