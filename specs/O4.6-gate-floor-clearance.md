# O4.6 Gate: floor clearance

## Requirement

> | `floor_clearance` | 0 samples with the tool point or any link origin below the floor plane | the bare plant has no contacts, so collision with the scene is checked geometrically |

From [OBJECTIVES.md §4 The score](../OBJECTIVES.md#4-the-score), ruler v1. OBJECTIVES.md is the frozen ruler and the one source of every number; this note explains it and never overrides it.

## What it means

The simulated arm has no contacts, so nothing physical stops it from swinging through the floor. The gate checks the
geometry instead: on no sample may the tool point or any link origin be below the floor plane (z = 0 at the base,
[O2.1](O2.1-deployment-ur5e-alone.md)). A point exactly on the floor does not count; a NaN pose fails.

## Why

"The bare plant has no contacts, so collision with the scene is checked geometrically" (OBJECTIVES.md §4). Without it a
solution could cut through the floor and score well doing it. Full collision detection is deferred
([O5.3](O5.3-collision-detection.md)).

## How it is checked

- `test_floor_*`: below counts and on the floor does not; samples are counted, not points; a custom floor height.
- `test_gate_floor_clearance*`: the gate reads the violation count, not a verdict's name.
