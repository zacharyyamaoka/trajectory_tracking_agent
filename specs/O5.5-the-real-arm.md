# O5.5 The real arm

## Requirement

> Deferred to ruler v2 (named so nobody thinks they were forgotten)
>
> the real arm (a separate stage a solution graduates into; never inside the search loop)

From [OBJECTIVES.md §5 Deferred to ruler v2](../OBJECTIVES.md#5-deferred-to-ruler-v2-named-so-nobody-thinks-they-were-forgotten), ruler v1. OBJECTIVES.md is the frozen ruler and the one source of every number; this note explains it and never overrides it.

## What it means

Everything here is simulation. A solution reaches a real arm only by graduating out of the search into a separate
stage; the search loop itself never drives hardware.

## Why

AGENTS.md: "Sim only. The real arm is a separate stage; nothing here opens a bus." Which line graduates first is in
[ideas.md](../ideas.md)'s *Suggestions for Zach* (the dispatcher's default: `p5s-kp2-pass2`, with `p5s-fric2-pass2` as
the fallback); flying it is a hardware step for Zach.

## How it is checked

Deferred: not part of ruler v1, so nothing tests it and its status is *deferred*. It becomes a requirement only in ruler v2, which OBJECTIVES.md says is a new section and a re-score of every solution in `solutions/`.
