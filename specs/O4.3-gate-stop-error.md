# O4.3 Gate: stop error ≤ 1 mm

## Requirement

> | `stop_error_max_mm` | ≤ 1.0 mm: target vs mean tool position over each stop's last 0.1 s (a stop = target at rest ≥ 0.2 s) | Zach's standing 1 mm tool gate (2026-11-11) |

From [OBJECTIVES.md §4 The score](../OBJECTIVES.md#4-the-score), ruler v1. OBJECTIVES.md is the frozen ruler and the one source of every number; this note explains it and never overrides it.

## What it means

A stop is any stretch where the target rests for at least 0.2 s. At each stop, average the measured tool position over
the stop's last 0.1 s and take its distance from the target; the task's number is its worst stop, and it must be at
most 1.0 mm. The mean lets a small zero-mean buzz average out; a steady offset does not. A task with no stops scores
0.0 and passes (CONTRACT §6).

## Why

Zach's standing 1 mm tool gate (2026-11-11), as OBJECTIVES.md names it. The objective rewards following the path while
it moves; this gate makes sure the arm also arrives. Filed as feedback:
[a 1 mm tool gate](../feedback/2026-11-11-tool-gate-1mm.md).

## How it is checked

- `test_stop_windows_*`: what a stop is, on both sides of the 0.2 s threshold, at the 50 Hz host rate, at the start
  and the end of a trajectory.
- `test_stop_error_*`: only the last 0.1 s counts; the worst stop against its own target; millimetres out of metres
  in; a zero-mean oscillation averages out; no stops is 0.0.
- `test_gate_stop_error_max_mm*`: the gate's threshold.
- `test_a_controller_that_never_moves_fails_the_stop_gate`: a real run that holds still fails it.
