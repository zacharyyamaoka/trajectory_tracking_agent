# O5.1 Speeds below 1.0

## Requirement

> Deferred to ruler v2 (named so nobody thinks they were forgotten)
>
> Speeds below 1.0 (the sweep)

From [OBJECTIVES.md §5 Deferred to ruler v2](../OBJECTIVES.md#5-deferred-to-ruler-v2-named-so-nobody-thinks-they-were-forgotten), ruler v1. OBJECTIVES.md is the frozen ruler and the one source of every number; this note explains it and never overrides it.

## What it means

Ruler v1 scores every task at speed 1.0 only ([O3.1](O3.1-dataset-workspace-v2.md)): the tapes are timed right at the
UR5e's limits. Slower runs of the same tasks (the sweep) are not scored.

## Why

Named in §5 "so nobody thinks they were forgotten". [ideas.md](../ideas.md)'s *Suggestions for Zach* asks for slower
speeds in ruler v2, so a gain at full speed can't hide a loss at low speed.

## How it is checked

Deferred: not part of ruler v1, so nothing tests it and its status is *deferred*. It becomes a requirement only in ruler v2, which OBJECTIVES.md says is a new section and a re-score of every solution in [`solutions/`](../solutions/).
