# What made p5s-pass3 good

The search condensed: one section per step of the target's lineage that changed a component, in the order the search took them, with what removing it from the end costs and what it does alone. Steps that changed no component are listed under *Not a component*. The builder adds every number from the ledger.

## + lookahead = bus delay — [p5s-bus](../solutions/p5s-bus/)

- key: `bus`
- idea: "Coulomb ff with the bus's own lookahead, not the calibration's"
- without it at the end: [abl-no-bus](../solutions/abl-no-bus/) (frozen) · [abl-no-bus-refit](../solutions/abl-no-bus-refit/) (refit)
- alone on the base: [p5s-bus](../solutions/p5s-bus/)

×20 worse when removed with everything else frozen, ×1.08 after one refit pass: the joint-error identification fits a torque proportional to qd, which is what a timing error looks like. Alone on p5s it is still the biggest single step (−39 %).

## + minus the board's lead — [p5s-bus-enc](../solutions/p5s-bus-enc/)

- key: `lead`
- idea: "Take the board's own lead out of the lookahead"
- without it at the end: [abl-no-lead](../solutions/abl-no-lead/) (frozen) · [abl-no-lead-refit](../solutions/abl-no-lead-refit/) (refit)
- alone on the base: [abl-p5s+lead](../solutions/abl-p5s+lead/)

×9.1 frozen, ×1.01 refit. With the bus lookahead it is one timing fix: removing both costs ×23.8, not the ×184 the two alone multiply to.

## + identified friction — [p5s-fric](../solutions/p5s-fric/)

- key: `fric`
- idea: "Identify per-joint Coulomb + viscous from dev run records"
- without it at the end: [abl-no-fric](../solutions/abl-no-fric/) (frozen) · [abl-no-fric-refit](../solutions/abl-no-fric-refit/) (refit)
- alone on the base: [abl-p5s+fric](../solutions/abl-p5s+fric/)

×3.0 frozen, ×1.007 refit: the later passes' friction basis spans it, so a refit re-learns it.

## + chord correction — [p5s-chord](../solutions/p5s-chord/)

- key: `chord`
- idea: "Move the position target off the chord"
- without it at the end: [abl-no-chord](../solutions/abl-no-chord/) (frozen)
- alone on the base: [abl-p5s+chord](../solutions/abl-p5s+chord/)

−0.6 % when the search took it, ×1.009 alone on p5s, ×1.45 when removed from the best: it only paid once the larger errors were gone. Not in the identification basis (it is a qdd term), so no refit was run.

## + gravity identification — [p5s-grav](../solutions/p5s-grav/)

- key: `grav`
- idea: "Identify the gravity error from the *joint errors*"
- without it at the end: [abl-no-grav](../solutions/abl-no-grav/) (frozen)
- alone on the base: [abl-p5s+grav](../solutions/abl-p5s+grav/)

−60 % when found; ×2.0 when removed from the best (frozen). Refit not measured; its 13 gravity shapes are inside passes 4 and 5's basis, so a refit would likely re-learn most of it, as it did for friction.

## + second friction pass — [p5s-fric2](../solutions/p5s-fric2/)

- key: `fric2`
- idea: "Second friction pass against the target velocity, from joint errors"
- without it at the end: [abl-no-fric2](../solutions/abl-no-fric2/) (frozen)
- alone on the base: [abl-p5s+fric2](../solutions/abl-p5s+fric2/)

×1.13 when removed: small now.

## + stiff gains (kp ×4) — [p5s-kp2](../solutions/p5s-kp2/), [p5s-kp4](../solutions/p5s-kp4/)

- key: `stiff`
- idea: "Stiffer board gains (kp ×2, kd ×√2) on top of the identified models"
- without it at the end: [p5s-pass3-g1](../solutions/p5s-pass3-g1/) (frozen) · [abl-no-stiff-refit](../solutions/abl-no-stiff-refit/) (refit)
- alone on the base: [abl-p5s+stiff](../solutions/abl-p5s+stiff/)

The one part a refit cannot replace: ×5.0 frozen (p5s-pass3-g1), ×2.6 after a refit at the boards' own gains. It is also the energy cost: +433 J over the dev tasks (+24 %). Transfer risk: the twin has no compliance.

## + velocity-lag term — [p5s-vlag](../solutions/p5s-vlag/)

- key: `vlag`
- idea: "Same joint-error identification for the inertia terms"
- without it at the end: [abl-no-vlag](../solutions/abl-no-vlag/) (frozen)
- alone on the base: [abl-p5s+vlag](../solutions/abl-p5s+vlag/)

×1.12 when removed: small now.

## + re-identification at kp ×4 — [p5s-pass2](../solutions/p5s-pass2/), [p5s-pass3](../solutions/p5s-pass3/)

- key: `reid`
- idea: "Re-identify friction + gravity from joint errors at the current (kp ×4) gains"
- without it at the end: [p5s-vlag](../solutions/p5s-vlag/) (frozen)
- alone on the base: [abl-p5s+reid](../solutions/abl-p5s+reid/)

×1.39 when removed (passes 4 and 5, fitted at kp ×4). p5s-onefit merged the passes into one model.json at equal error: 34 fewer runtime lines.

## Findings

- **Frozen, timing is most of it.** Removing the bus lookahead from p5s-pass3 makes it ×20.3 worse (0.636 mm), the board's lead ×9.1 (0.285 mm), both together ×23.8 (0.747 mm): one timing fix, not two (interaction ×0.13).
- **Refit, one identification pass re-learns it.** One `identify_pass.py` pass on each frozen variant's own dev runs: without the bus lookahead 0.0338 mm (×1.08), without the board's lead 0.0317 (×1.01), without identified friction 0.0316 (×1.007). Frozen leave-one-out overstates parts that a later fit can absorb.
- **Stiff gains are the part nothing replaces**: ×5.0 frozen, ×2.6 refit, and +24 % motor energy. Twin-only until it has compliance (ideas.md).
- **Late parts pay at the end.** The chord correction did −0.6 % when found and ×1.009 alone on p5s, yet removing it from the best costs ×1.45.
- **Greedy path (every step measured):** bus lookahead 0.595 → stiff gains 0.339 → board's lead 0.130 → friction 0.089 → gravity 0.078 → chord 0.053 → re-identification 0.036 → second friction pass 0.035 → velocity lag 0.031 mm. The first three give ×7.5 of the ×31.
- **A cheap point the search never visited:** bus lookahead + board's lead + stiff gains, `abl-path3+lead`: 0.130 mm in 23 runtime code lines with no identified model (the search's p5s-bus-enc: 0.393 mm in 20).
- **Method.** Fawcett & Hoos 2016: leave-one-out from the best, add-one-in from p5s, the greedy path. Path steps 2-7 were chosen on a surrogate (Biedenkapp et al. 2017; additive + Hamming-kernel ridge on log10 error, leave-one-out error ×1.56) and then measured; steps 1, 8 and 9 compared every candidate. Frozen = every other part keeps p5s-pass3's identified weights (`ablations/pass3-2026-11-24/make_variant.py`, which reproduces the 11 lineage solutions to ≤ 4e-12). 30 new solutions, all admissible, none better than p5s-pass3. Shapley values on the pages are model-based estimates.
